# Mandatory Rule: Zero Subtitle Descriptions Below Titles & Subtitles

## Core Guidelines & Mandates
1. **NO SUBTITLE PARAGRAPHS BELOW TITLES / SUBTITLES**:
   - Across **ALL** dashboards, menus, forms, cards, and modal components (covering Pegawai, Kepala Cabang, and Owner roles).
   - **STRICTLY PROHIBITED**: Adding `<p className="text-xs text-slate-400">`, `<p className="text-[11px]...">`, or small italic/explanatory tagline text directly underneath `<h1>`, `<h2>`, `<h3>`, `<h4>`, `<label>`, or section headers.
2. **CLEAN & ELEGANT UI DENSITY**:
   - Titles and section headers must remain clean, prominent, and concise without unnecessary text underneath.
   - Applies universally to all existing and future UI components, forms, cards, and dashboards.
